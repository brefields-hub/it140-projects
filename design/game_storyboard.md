# Project One Storyboard | Text-Based Adventure Game

## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**
Space Exploration / Sci-Fi Survival

**Storyline:**
You are an astronaut stranded on a space station whose primary core is malfunctioning. To survive and power the escape pod, you must navigate through the station, collect essential systems components, and avoid the rogue AI Overseer.

## Game Elements

**Rooms:**
1. Command Center (Start Room)
2. Oxygen Lab
3. Armory
4. Hydroponics
5. Science Lab
6. Medical Bay
7. Engine Room
8. Core Control (Villain Room)

**Items:**
1. Oxygen Tank
2. Plasma Cutter
3. Nutrient Pack
4. Research Notes
5. Medkit
6. Power Cell

**Villain:**
Rogue AI Overseer
